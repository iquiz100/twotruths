Before you start

Five quick things:

  • Yes means you actively want this.
  • Open means genuinely neutral-to-curious, not "I'll do it if they really want to." Any reluctance or dread is a No, not an Open.
  • The (fantasy) versions of Yes and Open are for fantasies only, you don't actually want to do this in real life.
  • No means you're not interested.
  • Never is a hard boundary that's a genuine relationship line for you.
