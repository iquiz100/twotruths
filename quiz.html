Before you start

Six quick things:

  • Yes means you actively want this.
  • Open means genuinely neutral-to-curious — not obligation, not "I'll do it if they really want to." If you feel any reluctance, dread, or "ehh, not really," that's a No, not an Open.
  • The (fantasy) versions of Yes and Open are for things that appeal to you as an idea, but that you don't actually want to do in real life.
  • No and Never both simply keep an item out of your shared results — your partner won't see which one you picked, or that you picked either.
  • Never exists so a hard boundary never gets mistaken for something worth gently revisiting. Use it for anything that's a genuine line for you.
  • Answer for yourself, honestly — not for what you think will please your partner or make a better match.
Reminder: Open means curious, not obligated.
